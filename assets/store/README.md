## Background ImageGen prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a NEW exclusive website background for a Pokémon trading-card shop, in the art style of Pokémon Ruby/Sapphire on Game Boy Advance. Create an orderly, cozy small-town CARD-SHOP STREET and courtyard in crisp, charming 16-bit top-down pixel art, a completely different setting from a beach, ocean or coastal inventory map. Landscape 3:2 composition, 1536x1024. This image sits BEHIND large opaque cream content panels: the middle 65% should be a very quiet pale warm stone pedestrian courtyard with extremely sparse large tiles and broad negative space, no focal character. Put the interesting scenery mostly along the OUTERMOST left and right 15% and the bottom corners. On the left edge, a small cream-and-wood trading card boutique with a muted ruby tiled roof and simple burgundy awning, a window showing a few colorful card backs/binders, a small circular Poké Ball emblem without letters. On the right edge, a tidy sage-green garden border, one wooden bench, a small lamp and another shop facade partly cropped by the edge. Small lavender flower planters near the lower corners, only a few. Warm afternoon daylight, soft muted cream, pale terracotta, dusty ruby, sage and soft blue-gray palette. The look is authentic GBA game environment tiles, sharp square pixel clusters, flat shaded sprites, limited color count, clean readable shapes. Peaceful, restrained, low contrast, plenty of breathing room. No ocean, no water, no beach sand, no pier, no boats, no repeated sparkle/confetti pattern, no treasure chests, no gold coins, no crowded market, no people or Pokemon crowds, no UI panels or buttons, no readable text, no letters, no watermark. Not an isometric 3D render, no painterly smoothing or photorealism. This must feel like a unique little card-shopping street, not the existing coastal storage-game background.

## Card-shop welcome illustration prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a wide decorative hero illustration for the real Pokémon trading-card storefront 'Bán Card Thui', used inside a small rectangular game dialogue window, around 500x220 CSS pixels; will also crop to a narrow portrait on phones. Create a charming Pokémon trading card shop interior in authentic Pokémon Ruby/Sapphire Game Boy Advance pixel art: crisp, deliberate low-resolution square pixels, limited warm cream, wood brown, sage green, sapphire navy and muted ruby red palette, no gradients or painterly detail. Straight-on slightly elevated top-down GBA view. A friendly Pikachu shopkeeper behind a small wooden counter in the CENTER, showing an open card binder to a Mudkip customer in front of the counter. Make their trading-card exchange immediately legible at small size. One neat glass card display with three collectible cards, one closed binder and a few organized booster packs. Small Poké Ball emblem as the shop decor. Cream walls and pale wood floor with ample quiet empty space around the central scene. Cozy, orderly, collector shop atmosphere. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, all important content grouped in the middle 50% of the image so it can crop to mobile, scene fills image edge to edge. No outdoor beach, no landscape, no gold coins, no treasure chests, no piles of clutter, no UI, no buttons, no readable text or letters, no watermark. Flat game sprite art with consistent pixel grid; no 3D render, no faux retro blur.
